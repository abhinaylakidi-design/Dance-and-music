A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Helpful answers.

Let’s make the next step feel easy.

How do I choose a service?

Start with your child’s interests and comfort. Trial & Program Guidance is a friendly first step if you are unsure.

Are these suitable for beginners?

Yes. We recommend an age-appropriate starting point and discuss any experience needed for a specific session.

How do bookings work?

Send an enquiry using the contact page. A place is confirmed only after the academy checks availability and shares the final fees.

Can siblings choose different services?

Yes. Each child can follow a different creative pathway. Ask the team about compatible schedules.
