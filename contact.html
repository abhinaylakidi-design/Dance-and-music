A LITTLE CLARITY

Questions are
always welcome.

Here are a few helpful starting points. For details about a specific program, send us an enquiry.

Ask your question
My child is a beginner. Where should we start?

Tell us their age and what they enjoy. Our team can discuss suitable starting options and explain what each class involves.

Can we visit before choosing a program?

Contact our team to arrange a visit. Please confirm the time before arriving so someone is available to welcome you.

How do I ask about an introductory class?

Choose the program you’re interested in on the enquiry form and mention that you would like an introductory class. Our team will confirm availability and any applicable fee.

Where can I find fees and class timings?

Fees and schedules depend on the program and age group. Contact the academy for current details before enrolling.

Can my child explore both dance and music?

Share both interests with us. We can discuss available programs and whether their schedules can work together.

What if my child needs extra support?

Let us know about any support, sensory, mobility, or learning needs when enquiring so we can discuss suitable arrangements.
