A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before their
first little step.

Choosing a class should feel clear and comfortable.

Talk to our team
Does my child need previous experience?

Many programs welcome beginners. Tell our team your child’s age and experience so we can discuss a suitable starting group.

Can my child explore both dance and music?

Yes. Ask about compatible class times and an age-appropriate balance that leaves room for rest, school, and practice.

How do I choose the right program?

Start with your child’s interests and use the program finder for ideas. Our team can discuss readiness, goals, placement, and available groups.

What should we bring to the first class?

Bring water and wear comfortable clothing. Ask the team about footwear, instruments, and any program-specific materials before attending.

Where can parents manage enrolled classes?

Use the parent dashboard to access enrollment, attendance, schedules, recital information, and fee payments for your child’s classes.
