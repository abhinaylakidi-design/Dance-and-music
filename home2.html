A LITTLE LOOK INSIDE

From “hello”
to “I did it!”

A thoughtful class rhythm helps children settle in, try something new, and leave with a moment to feel proud of.

Space to try. Time to grow.
  1. 01
    ARRIVE & CONNECT

    Find a familiar face.

    A welcoming check-in and playful warm-up make room for different moods and energy levels.

  2. 02
    DISCOVER & PRACTICE

    Try one new thing.

    Break a movement or musical idea into small, manageable steps with time to repeat and explore.

  3. 03
    CREATE & COLLABORATE

    Add their own sparkle.

    Use the new idea in a short sequence, a partner activity, or a creative challenge.

  4. 04
    REFLECT & CELEBRATE

    Notice the little wins.

    Finish with a calm moment to share what felt different, what was fun, and what to try next.

BEFORE THE FIRST HELLO

A few things
you might wonder.

Starting something new comes with questions. Here are a few simple pointers before you speak with our team.

Ask us a question
What if my child is still deciding?

Tell us what they enjoy at home, whether that is moving, singing, making sounds, or imagining stories. We can discuss suitable starting points and current options.

Do they need to practise before starting?

Beginner learning starts with the basics. Ask our team about the experience level and preparation needed for the particular class you are considering.

What should we bring to the first class?

Comfortable clothing and water are useful starting points. Confirm footwear, instruments, and any program-specific requirements with us before attending.

How do we choose a suitable class?

Share your child’s age, interests, previous experience, and availability. Contact the team to discuss suitable groups and the latest class schedule.
