A LITTLE LOOK INSIDE

From “hello”
to “I did it!”

A thoughtful class rhythm helps children settle in, try something new, and leave with a moment to feel proud of.

Space to try. Time to grow.
  1. 01
    ARRIVE & CONNECT

    Find a familiar face.

    A welcoming check-in and playful warm-up make room for different moods and energy levels.

  2. 02
    DISCOVER & PRACTICE

    Try one new thing.

    Break a movement or musical idea into small, manageable steps with time to repeat and explore.

  3. 03
    CREATE & COLLABORATE

    Add their own sparkle.

    Use the new idea in a short sequence, a partner activity, or a creative challenge.

  4. 04
    REFLECT & CELEBRATE

    Notice the little wins.

    Finish with a calm moment to share what felt different, what was fun, and what to try next.

TAKE THE CREATIVITY HOME

Small rituals.
Lovely discoveries.

Keep the joy going between classes with simple ideas that fit into family life. No special equipment needed.

01 / LISTEN

The kitchen orchestra

Find three safe household objects with different sounds. Take turns playing a pattern for the other person to copy.

A few playful minutes
02 / MOVE

The one-song adventure

Pick a favourite song. Move like the weather: a gentle breeze, a little rain, then a wonderful burst of sunshine.

One song, many ideas
03 / IMAGINE

A bedtime soundtrack

Add a soft sound or a small gesture to each character in a story. Let your child decide how the next page sounds.

A quieter kind of creativity
